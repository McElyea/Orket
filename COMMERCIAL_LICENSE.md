# Licensing transition

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Starting with the coordinated 0.8.0 release, Orket original code, SDK,
documentation, and templates use [Apache-2.0](LICENSE). Commercial use, hosting,
embedding, and redistribution are permitted subject to that license; the former
employee-count and competitive-offering restrictions do not apply to these versions.

Bundled third-party components retain their own terms in
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt).

Earlier releases retain the license and change-date commitments supplied with
those releases. Their historical commercial-licensing guidance remains in Git
history. This transition does not rewrite an existing signed agreement or imply
that older artifacts were released under Apache-2.0.

See [the licensing policy](docs/specs/LICENSING_POLICY.md) and
[the 0.8.0 release notes](docs/releases/0.8.0/RELEASE_NOTES.md).
